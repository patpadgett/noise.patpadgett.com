# Surface brief: index.html (the workstation)

Scope: the whole single-page app. Visitor mode: Operate. Audience: phonk producers making a beat in the browser; the task is play, tweak, chop, arrange, save. Proof/content: the demo phonk song itself, live and editable. Constraints: static GitHub Pages, Web Audio only, synthesized sounds, JSON + base64 WAV song file, detachable bottom editor.

## Direction contract (pinned by the owner; replaces the Select-O-Matic jukebox)

THESIS: NOISE is the dash of a Lamborghini Countach parked under a synthwave sun, and every gauge on it makes phonk. It refuses the dark 19-inch rack of brushed panels and screws (Reason played straight), and it refuses its own jukebox predecessor.

OWN-WORLD: Midnight-violet ground with a striped sun that rises only when the key is turned. Two lights: laser magenta and electric cyan; magenta is the melodic side (808, talkbox, master), cyan is the mechanical side (drums, bells, tape). Brushed chrome for everything the hand touches (keys, pads, piano whites); black LCD strips with cyan seven-segment text for everything that reads. A car ignition barrel (OFF / ACC / ON / START) is the audio unlock; a tachometer shows tempo; drum-reel odometers count bars; gear-shift levers run SCREW and SWING. Hero art: an in-house Flux render of a white Countach on wet neon asphalt (plate replaced with a PHONK plate); a pin-up poster rides the right column as the rack's Side A cover. Type: Audiowide for wordmarks, badges and readouts; Michroma for labels and tech text.

STORY: A producer turns the KEY. The dash lights, the sun comes up, lasers sweep the hero, and COUNTACH DRIFT (140 BPM, E minor, half-time drums, distorted sliding 808, cowbell hook, talkbox chants, chopped cassette) plays. Every chrome key they press changes it. They dub the song onto the CHOP SHOP's reel, cut it, load it into the TAPE DECK where the reels spin and the counter climbs. They detach the editor, arrange, and take the whole dash home as one readable JSON file.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Hero card with the Countach, wordmark, section nav, PHONK plate and NOW LOADED title. Below it the cluster: ignition + transport keys, tachometer, BPM/BAR/STEP odometers, SCREW/SWING levers, song LCD and file keys. Then the rack begins (V12 first) beside the pin-up poster.

DEVICES: V12 (drum machine), TRUNK (808), LAZERBELL (cowbell lead), TALKBOX (formant chants), TAPE DECK (slice player), CHOP SHOP (chopper), EXHAUST (master tape stage). Song-file device type ids stay the original ones (breaker, hearse, cathedral, preacher, carousel) so saved songs keep loading.

FINISH: reviewed in bounded passes with screenshots on desktop 1440 and mobile 390; DESIGN.md replaced; rasters carry provenance (assets/art/PROVENANCE.md and embedded prompts).
