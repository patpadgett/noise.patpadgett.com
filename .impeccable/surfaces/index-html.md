# Surface brief: index.html (the workstation)

Scope: the whole single-page app. Visitor mode: Operate. Audience: phonk producers making a beat in the browser; the task is play, tweak, chop, arrange, save. Proof/content: the demo phonk song itself, live and editable. Constraints: static GitHub Pages, Web Audio only, synthesized sounds, JSON + base64 WAV song file, detachable bottom editor.

## Direction contract

THESIS: NOISE is a late-night Memphis jukebox that makes phonk instead of playing it. The category default it refuses: the dark 19-inch rack of brushed panels, screws, and LED knobs (Reason played straight) and its predictable opposite, neon-cyberpunk dashboard glow.

OWN-WORLD: Black lacquered cabinet ground with chrome trim; every readable surface is a backlit cream title strip ruled in strip-red and strip-blue with hand-set type; chrome letter/number pushbuttons are the step grid; five inks only (lacquer black, cream, strip-red, strip-blue, bulb-amber) plus chrome. Bulb-amber is the only light and it follows transport state (dark idle, warm play, red-blue record). Physical digit readouts (drum counters) for BPM/bar/values. One speed lever moves every device. Type: a condensed grotesque for strip titles, a hand-set serif for song/slice names, tabular numerals in counters.

STORY: A producer arrives, drops a coin (INSERT COIN = audio unlock), the cabinet lights, the demo phonk track plays from the record carousel, and every strip they press changes it. They chop a loop on the LATHE, the slices become 45s in the CAROUSEL, the tone arm swings per slice. They pull the bottom editor off into its own window, arrange, and take the song home as a readable file.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width cabinet. Top: chrome header strip with NOISE in jukebox lettering, coin panel (INSERT COIN / PLAY / STOP), drum counters BPM · BAR · STEP, speed lever. Below: the device rack as stacked cabinet drawers, each a full-width invented device with cream title strips and chrome buttons; the CAROUSEL (slice player) shows the spinning 45s behind glass with the tone arm. Bottom: the block/piano-roll editor drawer with a DETACH chrome pull. Primary action: INSERT COIN, top-left, lit.

FORM: The Select-O-Matic jukebox, candidate 7 of 7 on my ordered list, assigned by seed key ff307b63 (mode operate). Raises: physical digit readouts (Nixie), one lever propagates (Miura), live editable in place with undo (HyperCard), five fixed inks (WPA), cabinet light follows state (Cyclorama).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
