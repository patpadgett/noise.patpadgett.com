Starting

Press INSERT COIN. The cabinet lights and the house record plays. Space plays and stops. Everything you hear is synthesized in the browser: there are no sample files, no account, no server.

The devices

BREAKER is the drum machine. The letter-and-number pushbuttons are the first bar of the current pattern: press one to write a hit. Right-click a pad to tune it up, shift-right-click to tune it down. MEMPHIS crushes it toward a fourth-generation cassette.

HEARSE is the 808. Notes that overlap slide; in the editor, shift-click a note to mark it SLIDE. COFFIN is the drive. COWBELL CATHEDRAL plays the melody; CHOIR stacks detuned bells, NAVE is the hall. PREACHER sings formant stabs; turn VOWEL while it plays.

Chopping

The LATHE cuts records. Drop any audio file on it, record the mic, or press RESAMPLE SONG to bounce the song itself (the loop if one is set, otherwise the first two bars). Cut by transients or evenly, drag cuts, click to add one, double-click to remove one, then PRESS TO CAROUSEL.

The CAROUSEL plays those slices: each slice is a 45 on the changer, and the tone arm swings to the one that fires. Shift-click a slice key to hear it reversed; in the editor shift-click a slice note to mark it REV.

Screw

The SCREW lever slows and pitches every device at once, the way a tape deck would. It is saved with the song.

Arranging

The editor at the bottom has two tabs. PATTERN is a piano roll (or a step grid for drums and slices). SONG lays pattern blocks across bars, one row per device. DETACH floats the editor in its own window; it stays in sync.

Saving

SAVE .JSON downloads the whole cabinet as one readable .noise.json file: tempo, every knob, every note, every block, and the carousel's audio as base64 WAV. OPEN or drag the file back to continue. WAV renders a mixdown. The cabinet also keeps an unsaved copy in this browser.
