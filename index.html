NOISE

Drop a song. Get phonk.

or paste a link

Everything happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. The song is split into stems, re-tuned into a minor key and re-chopped over a half-time beat with cowbells. No synth bass. Ever.

Real stem separation (optional, on a computer) downloads an 80 MB model once and takes a few minutes per song; a quick split plays in seconds.
