OWNER'S MANUAL

Ignition

Turn the KEY. The dash lights, the sun comes up, and COUNTACH DRIFT plays. Space plays and stops. Everything you hear is synthesized in your browser: no sample files, no account, no server.

The devices

V12 is the drum machine. The step grid is one bar of the current pattern (BAR 1 / BAR 2 pages the rest): tap a cell to write a hit, tap a lane's name to hear it. Tune a lane by right-clicking its name (shift for down), rolling the wheel over it, or with TUNE −/+ in the editor toolbar. MEMPHIS crushes the kit toward a dubbed cassette.

TRUNK is the 808: DRIVE pushes it through a clipper until the plate rattles. In the editor, shift-click a note (or select it and press SLIDE) to glide into it. LAZERBELL is the cowbell hook; STACK piles detuned bells, GRIT distorts them. TALKBOX chants formant syllables; turn VOWEL while it plays.

Chopping

The CHOP SHOP cuts tape. Drop any audio file on it, record the mic, or press RESAMPLE SONG to bounce the track itself (the loop if one is set, otherwise the first two bars). Cut by transients or evenly, drag cuts, click to add one, double-click to remove one, then LOAD TO DECK.

The TAPE DECK plays those slices: the reels spin, the counter climbs, and each slice lights its own segment. Shift-click a slice key to hear it reversed; in the editor shift-click a slice note, or select it and press REVERSE.

Screw

The SCREW lever slows and pitches every device at once, the way a tape deck losing battery would. It is saved with the song.

Arranging

The editor at the bottom has two tabs. PATTERN is a piano roll (or a step grid for drums and slices); tap a cell to write, tap a note to select, drag to move, drag its right edge to stretch. SONG lays pattern blocks across bars, one row per device. Wide grids scroll with the wheel, the scrollbar, or a finger. DETACH floats the editor in its own window; it stays in sync. HOW lists every gesture.

Saving

SAVE JSON downloads the whole dash as one readable .noise.json file: tempo, every knob, every note, every block, and the deck's audio as base64 WAV. OPEN or drag the file back to continue. WAV renders a mixdown. An unsaved copy also stays in this browser.
